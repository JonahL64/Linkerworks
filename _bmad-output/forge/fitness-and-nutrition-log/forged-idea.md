# Hardened direction

- Replace the passive Trackers concept with an action-first Log area; meals and workouts are independent dated records in the existing App Group SwiftData store.
- Nutrition begins with manual macro entry and editable daily targets; it does not include a food database or API.
- Training begins with a start/finish workout flow, exercises, and completed sets; it does not include routines, coaching, or HealthKit.
- Scale navigation by user intent: Today, Plan, Log, Progress, and More.
