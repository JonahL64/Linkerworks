HARDENED

Fitness and nutrition log

Linkerworks should grow by intent, not by adding a tab for every specialist tool.

Locked decisions

  • Meals and workouts are first-class, dated records in the existing shared SwiftData store.
  • Nutrition starts with manual macro entry and daily targets.
  • Training starts with sessions, exercises, and set logging.
  • Navigation becomes Today, Plan, Log, Progress, and More.

Rejected for now

  • Food databases, barcode scanning, nutrition APIs, HealthKit, workout plans, coaching, and social features.

Surviving weak point

“Trackers” conflates passive history/reference material with active tools. Move the former into each tool or Progress; do not preserve Trackers as the main entry point.

Pressure-tested by Winston (system architect) and Mara, a skeptical personal-training-app user.
