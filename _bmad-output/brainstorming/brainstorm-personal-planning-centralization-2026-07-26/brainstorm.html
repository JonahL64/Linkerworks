01

Draw the load-bearing lines.

First Principles Thinking asked what a personal operating system must do before it earns any new specialist feature.

The front door⌁

A universal capture inbox

Any task, event, assignment, note, meal idea, or workout thought begins in one neutral place, then gets classified later.

The connective tissue◎

Areas, Projects, Goals

Health, School, Career, Home, Money, and Relationships become stable context. Projects organize finite outcomes; goals hold slower change.

The daily surface☀

One Today brief

Routine tasks, due assignments, active workout, calendar agenda, and one chosen goal focus become a coherent opening view.

The weekly rhythm↻

A guided review

What changed? What slipped? What is blocked? What should actually be scheduled next?

The memory≋

Search and activity timeline

Find anything, then see what was planned, completed, skipped, logged, or changed on any day.

The trust contract◇

Readable export and verified restore

Before the app becomes home to irreplaceable life data, it must prove that data can leave—and return—through durable JSON and CSV.

02

Design the failure. Then turn it around.

Reverse Brainstorming exposed the fastest ways to ruin trust, truth, and usability.

A top-level tracker for every interest

Organize additions under a few stable Areas.

Trap all data in SwiftData

One-tap export, restore rehearsal, readable archives.

Require a full form for every thought

Inbox-first capture and progressive detail.

Recompute the past from today’s plan

Snapshot every schedule-sensitive metric.

Make every miss a failure

Skip, defer, renegotiate, and explain.

Call parsing a release test

A real shared test scheme and device acceptance checklist.

03

Cut, combine, adapt, reverse.

SCAMPER treated existing Linkerworks patterns as raw material—not constraints on imagination.

Substitute

Capture now, classify later

Replace category selection with a neutral inbox item that can be promoted when context is available.

Combine

One agenda, distinct semantics

Events and assignments can share a view without pretending due and completion mean the same thing.

Adapt

Quick capture presets

Extend saved-meal convenience to events, assignments, and journal prompts.

Modify

A three-outcome Focus strip

Place a compact, manually chosen set above routine work in Today.

Eliminate

Retire duplicate tracker roots

Let each Area own its references, logs, goals, and review metrics.

Reverse

Plan from capacity

Start with available time and energy rather than forcing every desire into a date.

05

Borrow wisdom from other worlds.

Cross-Pollination imported proven mental models, then translated them into humane personal tools.

Review, then prioritize

An inbox item receives urgency, importance, and a next action only when reviewed—not while capturing.

An honest mutation trail

Meaningful changes append lightweight audit events, making history and undo trustworthy.

Scenario planning

Duplicate a plan into a safe possibility before applying changes to the live week.

Backup before the leap

Create explicit local snapshots before migrations or large bulk edits.

The morning meeting

A concise brief: deadlines, conflicts, exceptions, and one decision needed.

Periodize ambition

Goals move through build, maintain, deload, and recovery instead of demanding constant maximum effort.

06

The edges sharpen the product.

Constraint Mapping turned local-only, single-user, iPhone-first, and finite maintenance time into decisive product principles.

Because it is local-only

Files export and import become the primary portability story—not an afterthought.

Because it is single-user

Terminology, defaults, and views can fit Jonah’s mental model instead of generic collaboration roles.

Because there are no notifications

Approaching items surface in widgets, Today, and a deliberate morning review.

Because iPhone is primary

Fast capture, one-thumb actions, and short loops outrank dense planning screens.

Because privacy is central

Local app lock and sensitive widget redaction become natural safeguards.

Because maintenance is finite

A new module must improve Today or Review before it earns a standalone screen.

07

A letter from the person who kept using it.

The Future Self Interview shifted the value proposition from perfect tracking to durable, meaningful memory.

Some year after the checkmarks stopped feeling urgent
Dear present Jonah,

I don’t need a perfect archive of tiny completions. I need to find the decisions that changed things—and remember why I made them.

Give me annual summaries of what I tried, finished, learned, enjoyed, and chose to stop. Let me compare plans with reality. Preserve the seasons: semesters, jobs, injuries, certifications, moves.

Keep exports readable even if Linkerworks no longer compiles. Save the highlights too, so the archive carries feeling, not only evidence. And when a goal ends, package its outcomes, lessons, and reusable patterns instead of burying it.
— Future Jonah
08

Remove the defaults. What survives?

What If Scenarios stripped away familiar surfaces to expose what the system is actually for.

?

?

If the calendar vanished: let weekly review and horizon buckets drive planning without false precision.

?

If streaks vanished: show consistency range, trend direction, and recovery after disruption.

?

If the phone vanished today: make portable encrypted backup the first feature, not housekeeping.

?

If school ended tomorrow: hide Homework cleanly and preserve it as an archived life chapter.

?

If planning took ten minutes weekly: guide a queue toward a realistic week and an empty inbox.

The build order

Foundation first

Verified build and test, aligned iOS target, versioned migrations, consistent save errors, and export/restore. Trust precedes expansion.

Open the front door

Universal Inbox, global search, and destination-specific deep links. Capture becomes fast; retrieval becomes certain.

Add the operating layer

Areas, Projects, Goals, and lightweight links to tasks, events, assignments, certifications, workouts, and logs.

Establish the rhythm

A focused Today brief, guided weekly review, reusable templates, and planned exception modes for real life.

Only then: consider journal, mood, sleep metrics, finances, household inventory, relationship follow-ups, or experiments. A specialist module earns its place by strengthening the shared operating layer—not by adding another island.
